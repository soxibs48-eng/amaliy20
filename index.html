Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining Coursely and launching your podcast.

Do I need expensive recording gear before starting? +

Not at all! You can start with a standard $60 USB microphone (like the Samson Q2U) and free software. Module 2 breaks down smart equipment choices for every budget so you never overpay.

How much time do I need to commit each week? +

The course is designed for busy professionals. Expect to spend 3 to 4 hours per week watching lessons and completing hands-on assignments. All content is lifetime access, so you can also learn at your own pace.

Will I get feedback on my pilot episode? +

Yes! Pro members receive a direct audio review from our senior audio engineers. Self-Paced members can also post episode drafts in our peer community forum for critique.

What if I've never edited audio before? +

We built Module 4 specifically for absolute beginners. We provide step-by-step screen recordings and downloadable audio presets for Audacity, GarageBand, and Descript so you sound amazing right away.

Is there a satisfaction guarantee? +

Absolutely. We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. If you complete the first two modules and feel it isn’t right for you, simply email us for a full refund.
