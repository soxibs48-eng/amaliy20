## 🎨 Loyiha haqida

Ushbu loyiha Figma platformasidagi **Coursely** andozasi (node-id: `31-1428`) asosida to'liq va mukammal ishlab chiqilgan bo'lib, podkast yaratish, audio montaj qilish, Apple Podcasts & Spotify platformalariga tarqatish hamda monetizatsiya qilish bo'yicha onlayn masterklass veb-sahifasini ifodalaydi.

### 📐 Asosiy bo'limlar:
1. **Header & Sticky Navbar**:
   - `Coursely` brendi (Blue dot & Masterclass badjisi)
   - Dinamik silliq navigatsiya havolalari
   - `Enroll Now` aksiyador tugmasi va mobil hamburger menyu.
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Asosiy sarlavha: *"Learn How To Launch A Successful Podcast"*
   - Podkaster ayolning studiyadagi yuqori sifatli fotosurati
   - Figmaning o'ziga xos geometrik rangli kvadratlari (ko'k va marjon aksentlar)
   - *"Watch Trailer"* va *"Enroll Now"* tugmalari, 4.9/5 yulduzli reyting dalili.
3. **Core Advantages (3 Card)**:
   - *Structured Roadmap*
   - *Studio Production*
   - *Growth & Monetization*
4. **Split Story Section 1 (Mentorlik)**:
   - Mentor (David Ross) fotosurati va *"Learn with experts and take your podcast elsewhere"* bloki.
5. **Split Story Section 2 (Studio texnikasi)**:
   - Professional audio miksher va mikrofon fotosurati va *"Get the abilities and tools you need to create"* bloki.
6. **What's Included (Yorqin elektr-ko'k banner)**:
   - Skroll vaqtida avtomatik hisoblovchi animatsion raqamlar:
     - **6** Modules · **50+** Videos · **20 hrs** Content · **4** Mentors · **50k** Community
7. **The Course Curriculum (Interaktiv akkordeon)**:
   - 6 ta modulning darslar va daqiqalari bilan to'liq ro'yxati (ochilish/yopilish animatsiyasi).
8. **By the end of the 4-week course... (8 ta ko'nikma kartasi)**:
   - Kurs yakunida o'quvchi ega bo'ladigan barcha amaliy mahoratlar (2x4 zamonaviy panjara).
9. **Dark Navy Quote Spotlight**:
   - To'q ko'k rangli iqtibos bloki va Sarah Jenkins sharhi.
10. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ akkordeon)**:
    - 5 ta eng ko'p beriladigan savol va javoblar.
11. **Our Plans & Cost for You (Marjon rangli narxlar bloki)**:
    - *Self-Paced* ($149) va *Pro Mentored* ($299, Most Popular) tarif kartalari.
12. **Word From Previous Students (6 ta talaba sharhlari)**:
    - 5 yulduzli sharhlar, real fotosuratlar va podkast nomlari.
13. **Footer**:
    - Sayt xaritasi, ijtimoiy tarmoqlar (X, YouTube, Spotify, LinkedIn) va haftalik elektron xabarnomaga obuna bo'lish formasi.

## 🛠 Texnologiyalar to'plami

- **HTML5**: Semantik teglash (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
- **CSS3**: Vanilla CSS, maxsus dizayn tokenlari (CSS Custom Variables), Flexbox, CSS Grid, Glassmorphism, Box-shadow effektlari.
- **JavaScript (Vanilla ES6+)**:
  - Sticky header effekti
  - Mobil menyu drawer
  - Akkordeonlar (Curriculum va FAQ)
  - Interaktiv modal oynalar (Video treyler va Ro'yxatdan o'tish)
  - Skrollda ishga tushuvchi raqamlar hisoblagichi (IntersectionObserver)
  - Toast bildirishnomalari
  - "Back to Top" silliq qaytish tugmasi.
